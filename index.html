FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Some common questions about our application assistance service.

Is this a government website?

No. This website provides independent private application-assistance/intake services. It is not an official government website.

Does submitting this form guarantee a visa?

No. Visa or immigration decisions are made by the relevant government authority. Submission of an assistance request does not guarantee approval.

How can I track my application?

After submission, you receive an application number. Enter that number in the Track Application section.

Can I check official government information?

Yes. We recommend checking the official government websites for current eligibility, fees, documents, processing information and application procedures.

Can I contact you through WhatsApp?

Yes. Our current WhatsApp contact is +1 249 477 1916.
